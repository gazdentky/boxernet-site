## About Us

Boxernet Inc. is an **IoT company that manages the "state transitions" of urban infrastructure**. We detect the state of urban infrastructure scattered across the city — trash bins, collection points, parking spaces, and various lockers — using a range of sensors, and estimate current status, demand, and anomalies with proprietary algorithms such as the Hidden Markov Model (HMM). We plan, develop, and operate services and applications that derive optimal operations remotely.

We are not a single-product company. We are a platform company that deploys purpose-specific services on top of a common foundation called "state-transition management."

### Main Service Lineup

- **Tsubusukun** — A smart recycling box dedicated to PET bottles (an automatic compression mechanism plus sensors remotely manage fill levels and collection timing)
- **Smart Trash Bin** — A fill-detection / collection-optimization solution for general and sorted waste
- **Pickup Station** — Operating and managing handover points for e-commerce parcels and recyclables on a state-transition basis
- **Smart Coin Parking** — Visualizing the usage state of parking spaces in real time to support utilization and pricing optimization
- **State-Transition Management Platform** — A common platform spanning the above services (sensors × HMM × cloud management)

Through these, we contribute to the efficiency of urban infrastructure — resource collection, logistics, and mobility — and to the realization of a sustainable society.
